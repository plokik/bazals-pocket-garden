# Malovaná kočka – grafické zadání

Režim: vestavěný `imagegen`, skutečné průhledné pozadí. Produkční PNG zůstávají beze změny; Godot používá samostatně změřené výřezy jednotlivých siluet.

## cat_poses_v1.png

Create a transparent animation sprite sheet of one friendly ginger tabby garden cat, cream chest and paws, green eyes, recognizable orange stripes. Match Bazal's Pocket Garden: richly hand-painted mobile game illustration, strong dark olive-brown outlines, warm highlights from the upper left, soft painted shading, readable silhouettes, no pixel art, no photorealism, no baked background. Full body in every pose. Four columns and three rows: four right-facing walking phases; sitting, crouching to lie down, lying awake, curled up asleep; a deep stretch, yawning, licking a front paw, grooming its shoulder. Keep the same cat design and physical scale throughout. No text, collars, clothes, props or grid lines. Real transparent alpha around every silhouette.

## cat_corner_v1.png

Create one integrated small resting corner for that painted garden cat, on a truly transparent background. Empty oval honey-brown wicker bed on the right, soft sage-green cushion, exactly two small matching green ceramic bowls in front on the left: blue water and brown cat kibble. Warm upper-left lighting, glossy painted shapes and strong dark olive-brown contours matching Bazal's Pocket Garden. Front three-quarter view, believable furniture scale, subtle contact shadows. No cat, floor, room, labels, checkerboard or solid background. Keep the whole bed and both bowls within the image.

## cat_actions_v1.png

Using cat_poses_v1.png as the reference, draw the exact same ginger tabby cat as a transparent four-column, three-row animation sheet. Row one: four successive phases of licking a raised front paw and washing its face. Row two: four successive phases of yawning, mouth closed, opening, fully open, closed again. Row three: four successive phases of turning its head, licking its shoulder/body and lifting its head again. Maintain the same painted contours, cream markings, green eyes, stripe pattern, warm lighting and body scale. Full silhouettes, no text, no background, actual transparent alpha. Keep feet on a shared baseline within each action.

Zadání jsou zde shrnuta pro další výtvarné iterace. Každý atlas má 12 póz; celkem hra používá 24 snímků. Dlouhé klidové pauzy jsou v předváděcím GIFu zkrácené, herní rychlost chůze a jednotlivých akcí zůstává běžná.
